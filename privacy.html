Common questions

Questions people ask us.

Will MindLoom show me things I'm not allowed to see?

No. MindLoom follows the access rules already set in your tools. If you can't open something there, you won't see it in MindLoom.

Does MindLoom record my screen?

Only if you turn on MindLoom Capture, and only the screenshots you approve. You can hide parts of the screen or throw a screenshot away before anything is uploaded.

What happens if an answer is wrong?

Every answer shows its sources so you can check it. Experts approve answers before they become company knowledge, conflicting sources are flagged for review, and admins can fix or remove any answer.

Do we have to replace our current tools?

No. MindLoom connects to the email, chat, calendars, drives and meeting tools you already use. There is nothing to migrate.

Who decides which information is included?

Your administrators. They choose which inboxes, folders and channels to connect, can preview them first, and can pause a connection at any time.

Can MindLoom take actions on its own?

Not without a person. Today it drafts messages and proposed changes for someone to approve. When workflow automation arrives, it will still stop and ask before any important step.
