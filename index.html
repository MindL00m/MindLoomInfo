The problem

Eight reasons company knowledge breaks.

Every organisation has the same gaps. They cost time every day and hurt most when people leave.

Scattered everywhere

Spread across email, chat, drives and tools, and walled off between teams.

Never written down

Judgement and know-how live only in people's heads.

Walks out the door

When people leave, years of experience leave with them.

Decisions lose their why

The decision is recorded. The reasoning behind it is not.

Hard to trust

Old, duplicated and conflicting versions sit side by side.

Experts are hidden

Job titles don't show who has solved this problem before.

No time to document

Writing things up always loses to real work.

Nobody owns it

No one keeps it up to date, so it quietly goes stale.

Who feels it

Everyone pays, in a different way.

  • Learn by interrupting others

Employees

Experts and managers
