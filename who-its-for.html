Who it's for

For everyone who needs an answer, and everyone who keeps giving one.

MindLoom helps the whole organisation, from someone on their first day to the people running the company.

What changes

Before and after MindLoom.

New starters
TodayLearn by interrupting colleagues and waiting for replies. It takes months to feel confident.
With MindLoomAsk Loombot from day one and get answers with sources, plus the name of the person who knows more.
Employees
TodaySearch tool after tool, then redo work that someone else already finished.
With MindLoomOne answer, the next step, and past work to build on, all in one place.
Experts and managers
TodayAnswer the same questions every week and chase follow-ups buried in email.
With MindLoomAnswer once, even by voice note. It becomes company knowledge, and follow-ups show up on the status board.
Leaders
TodayUpdates arrive late and filtered through several layers. New projects start from zero.
With MindLoomA live view of open projects and issues with the evidence behind them. New projects start with a ready-made brief.
