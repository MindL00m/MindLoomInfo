Agent 1 · Learns

Weaver

Brings in what's written down, and learns what isn't.

Solves: knowledge scattered across tools, know-how that was never written down, no time to document, experience leaving with people.

Connects to your tools

Available now

Gmail, Google Drive and Calendar, Outlook, Teams, SharePoint, Zoom, Slack, GitHub and the staff directory. Each person chooses what to include. An admin connects Slack for the organisation.

MindLoom Capture

Available now

A Mac app and a Chrome extension. On a Mac you allowlist the apps, and only a task summary is uploaded, not screenshots or what you type. In Chrome, screenshots stay on the device until you approve them.

Asks the expert for you

Available now

When the brain doesn't know, it finds the right colleague and sends the question by Gmail, Outlook or Teams. Nothing goes out until you confirm.

Answer once, in any format

Available now

MindLoom Capture

Know-how that was never written down, captured without the paperwork.

People choose which Mac apps or Chrome tabs to include. MindLoom drafts the steps and the judgement behind them, and an expert approves the result before anyone else can follow it.

1. Choose what to shareOn a Mac, allowlist the apps. In Chrome, capture is the active tab, and you approve each screenshot.
2. Map the stepsMindLoom drafts a Skill File: the steps, tools and hand-offs.
3. Learn the exceptionsIt notes warnings, judgement calls and questions to ask the expert.
4. Expert approvesOnce approved, people who are allowed to see it can follow it as a Mac guide.

Private by default: the Mac app uploads a task summary only when you end a session you started. Screenshots upload only after you approve them. Raw Mac events stay on the Mac.
