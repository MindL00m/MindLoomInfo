MindLoom Capture

Know-how that was never written down, captured without the paperwork.

Employees choose which screens to share. MindLoom works out the steps and the judgement behind them, and an expert approves the result.

1. Share what you chooseApprove each screenshot and hide anything confidential first.
2. Map the stepsMindLoom works out the steps, tools and hand-offs.
3. Learn the exceptionsIt notes warnings, judgement calls and questions to ask the expert.
4. Expert approvesOnce approved, it becomes a guide anyone can follow.
